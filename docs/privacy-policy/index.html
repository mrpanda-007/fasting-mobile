Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Fasting is designed to work without an account or remote sync. Your fasting information stays on your device. We do not sell, share, or transmit it to our servers.

Information stored on your device

To provide the timer and history features, the app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • fasting plans, including names and selected durations;
  • fast and refeed start, target, and end timestamps, plus completion status and history;
  • an identifier for an optional local notification scheduled for an active phase;
  • your display-theme preference; and
  • if you use the currently local-only partner-preview flow, the partner name and role you save in that preview.

This information is used only to show your timer, history, preferences, and Android home-screen widget. It is not sent to us or to a third party by the app.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you allow them, the app schedules a local notification on your device when a fast or refeed target is reached. This is not a push-notification service: no notification content, token, or fasting information is sent to us.

Android widget

If you add a Fasting widget to your Android home screen, Android stores the current plan name, phase, timestamps, cycle, and selected theme locally so the widget can display them when the app is closed. This widget information is not transmitted by the app.

Information we do not collect or share

The current version of Fasting does not create user accounts or use remote sync. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, cookies, a crash-reporting service, or a remote database. It does not sell or share your fasting information.

Retention and deletion

Your local information remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. The in-app Delete all local data action removes local fasting records and preferences, clears widget information, and cancels scheduled local notifications.

Android backup is enabled. If you choose to use your device's backup service, Android may copy app data to the backup provider associated with your device. We do not receive or control those backup copies; manage or remove them through your device and backup-provider settings.

Children

Fasting is intended for adults who choose to fast. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. We will update the date above and publish the revised policy at this address. Any future account, sync, analytics, advertising, or third-party data feature will be described here before it is released.
